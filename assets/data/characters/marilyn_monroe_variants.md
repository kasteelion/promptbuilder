# Marilyn Monroe: Framework Testing (FINAL VERDICT)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **VERDICT: Variant E (Tokenized Standard) Won.**
>
> - **Kibbe (A):** Too abstract; lost the Caucasian identity entirely (produced Image 1).
> - **Somatotype (C):** Too weighted; produced a plus-size result (Image 3).
> - **Tokenized (E):** Captured the exact structural "blueprint" and core features (Image 5).

### Marilyn Variant A (Kibbe Romantic)

**Medium/Format:**
High-End Digital Photography (85mm Portrait Lens simulation).

**Technical Specifications:**
f/1.8 aperture for shallow depth of field. Sharp focus on eyes with smooth fall-off. Global illumination, accurate ray-traced reflections, and true-to-life skin texture (pores, vellus hair, subsurface scattering). 8K resolution.

**Stylistic Rules:**
Neutral, balanced color grading. No stylization filters. Lighting is soft, diffused studio quality (soft-box or window light). Composition is clean and centered.

**Negative Prompt:**
Illustration, painting, drawing, cartoon, anime, heavy grain, low contrast, distorted, makeup smudge.

- **Body:** Official Romantic type; lush, rounded silhouette with high Yin energy; soft, fleshy contours; lack of vertical line; projects a "delicate but curved" presence.
- **Face:** Luminous facial features; round, large bedroom eyes; full, sensuous lips; rounded chin and jawline; soft planes.
- **Hair:** Platinum blonde; lush, high-volume Type 1-2 waves; mid-length.
- **Skin:** Fair, luminous porcelain skin; warm ivory undertones.
- **Outfit**
- **Top:** White Wool Off-shoulder Crop Top
- **Bottom:** High-waist light-wash denim shorts or athletic joggers in soft gray
- **Footwear:** White chunky sneakers or pastel platform trainers
- **Accessories:** Small hoop earrings, delicate silver chain necklace
- **Hair/Makeup:** Messy high bun with face-framing tendrils
  **SCENE/SETTING:**
  Studio Simple Background
